# BrickVerse Engine

BrickVerse Engine is the technology powering **BrickVerse Client** and **BrickVerse Creator**, built to provide a modern user-generated content platform where developers can build, script, publish, and eventually earn from their creations.

## Our Origins

We believe in being transparent about where BrickVerse Engine came from.

BrickVerse Engine **originally began as a fork of the Polytoria (PT) engine**.

The reason was straightforward: **we didn't want to reinvent the wheel just for the sake of reinventing it.**

When we started building BrickVerse, PT already provided a useful technical foundation for many of the basic systems a UGC platform needs. Rather than spending an enormous amount of development time recreating functionality that already existed, we chose to start with that foundation and focus our efforts on where we believed we could take it.

That gave us a head start — **not a finish line**.

Since that original fork, we've spent countless hours modifying, extending, replacing, and building new systems throughout the engine. What started as a PT fork has increasingly developed into the **BrickVerse Engine**, with its own features, APIs, tooling, platform integrations, and technical direction.

We don't hide the engine's history, but its origins shouldn't be confused with its current state.

**BrickVerse today is far more than the codebase we originally forked.**

## From a Fork to Our Own Engine

Forking an existing project doesn't mean development stops at changing a name, logo, or interface.

For us, it meant having a foundation that we could immediately begin building upon.

Since then, our focus has been on **maturing the engine** into something capable of supporting the larger BrickVerse ecosystem.

We've continued introducing and expanding systems such as:

* Enhanced physics and constraints
* Attachments, trails, and beams
* PBR materials and `SurfaceAppearance`
* Improved lighting and post-processing
* Highlighting and rendering features
* Custom shader capabilities
* Expanded UI components and effects
* Improved audio systems
* Better interaction APIs
* Vehicle and physics functionality
* Parallel Luau capabilities
* Expanded DataModel classes
* New developer APIs
* Asset and publishing infrastructure
* Creator-focused development tooling

Some systems still share ancestry with PT. Others have been substantially changed, replaced, or created specifically for BrickVerse.

And that process continues with every release.

## What Makes BrickVerse Different?

Our goal isn't to maintain a PT clone.

We're building a complete creation platform with our own direction for what a UGC engine should become.

### A More Capable Engine

We're continuously expanding what developers can build inside BrickVerse.

That means improving the fundamentals — physics, rendering, scripting, networking, UI, audio, assets, and the DataModel — while also introducing functionality that didn't exist in the engine we originally started with.

We're especially focused on giving developers more control rather than keeping the engine limited to basic building and scripting.

As BrickVerse grows, we expect the technical differences between the original foundation and the modern BrickVerse Engine to continue becoming larger.

### Developer Tooling

The engine itself is only one part of development.

**BrickVerse Creator** is being developed alongside it with tooling for scripting, asset management, publishing, world and universe management, debugging, and other development workflows.

We're also building **Forge**, our AI-assisted development tooling designed around the BrickVerse development environment.

Our goal is to make BrickVerse approachable for someone building their first experience while still providing increasingly advanced tools for developers who want to push the engine further.

## More Than an Engine

BrickVerse isn't intended to simply be an engine with a website attached to it.

We're building an interconnected ecosystem:

**BrickVerse Creator → BrickVerse Engine → BrickVerse Client → BrickVerse Platform**

Developers can create worlds, upload assets, write Luau code, publish experiences, manage universes, build communities, and distribute their creations through the same ecosystem.

We're investing in the infrastructure surrounding the engine just as much as the engine itself.

## Building a Creator Economy

Another major part of our long-term direction is giving developers an opportunity to **earn from what they create**.

We're exploring programs such as **developer exchange (DevEx) and creator revenue sharing**, where eligible creators could eventually receive real-world compensation for the value and activity their experiences bring to BrickVerse.

Building something like this responsibly involves financial, regulatory, compliance, fraud-prevention, and platform-sustainability considerations. Because of that, we're taking the time to design these programs properly rather than promising an immediate payout system.

But the principle behind it is important to us:

> **If developers help build the experiences that make BrickVerse successful, we want developers to have an opportunity to share in that success.**

## We're Still Building

BrickVerse Engine isn't finished, and we're not going to pretend otherwise.

There are inherited systems we're continuing to improve or replace. There are APIs that need to mature. There are features we still want to build, and there will be decisions we revisit as the platform grows.

That's part of developing an engine.

We're also not going to pretend BrickVerse Engine appeared out of nowhere.

**Yes, we forked PT. We did it because rebuilding an existing foundation from zero would've meant spending valuable development time reinventing the wheel instead of building what we actually wanted BrickVerse to become.**

That foundation allowed us to move faster.

What defines BrickVerse isn't where the first line of code came from — it's the countless hours of engineering that have happened since, the systems we're building today, and where we're taking the platform next.

## Our Direction

BrickVerse is being built around a few core ideas:

* Give developers increasingly powerful creation tools.
* Keep expanding what experiences can do.
* Build a cohesive engine, Creator, Client, and web ecosystem.
* Make UGC development approachable without unnecessarily limiting advanced developers.
* Provide modern APIs and development tooling.
* Build sustainable ways for successful creators to earn from their work.
* Continue replacing, improving, and expanding the original engine foundation.
* Let the engine develop its own identity through the technology we build for it.

We're proud of how BrickVerse Engine started, but we're much more interested in **what it's becoming**.
